# 💡 More Features — Ideas to Make the App Better

> These are future features that could make Delphic AI smarter, more useful, and more fun to use.
> Ordered from most important (top) to least important (bottom).
> Pick any one and we can build it together!

---

## 🥇 High Priority — Makes the App Much More Useful

| Feature | What it does | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **Conversation memory across sessions** | Right now the chat resets when you refresh. This would save your past conversations using a database (SQLite) | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Check my work** | Student types their own answer and the AI checks if it's right, and explains where they went wrong | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Hint mode** | Instead of giving the full answer, the AI gives one hint at a time. Student has to ask for the next hint | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Show steps for SymPy solutions** | Right now SymPy just gives the answer — show the actual algebra steps it took | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Difficulty level selector** | A toggle: *Middle School / High School / College* — changes how the AI explains things | ⭐ Easy |

---

## 🥈 Medium Priority — Nice Improvements

| Feature | What it does | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| **"Explain differently" button** | A button under each answer that re-asks the AI with a different approach | ⭐ Easy |
| **Practice quiz mode** | The AI generates 5 practice problems on a topic and grades your answers | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Multiple functions on one graph** | Plot `sin(x)` and `cos(x)` on the same graph to compare them | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Formula sheet** | A built-in cheat sheet of common formulas (quadratic, trig identities, etc.) | ⭐ Easy |
| **PDF export** | Download any AI answer as a clean PDF (great for homework) | ⭐⭐ Medium |
| **Unit converter** | Type "convert 5km to miles" and get a clean answer | ⭐ Easy |
| **Daily challenge** | One new math problem every day on the home screen | ⭐⭐ Medium |
