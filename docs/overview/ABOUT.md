# Delphic AI — Product Overview

## One-Line Summary

Delphic AI is a focused learning workspace that helps students reason through problems step by step instead of only receiving final answers.

## What It Does Today

Delphic AI currently works best as a math tutoring assistant. It can:

- explain solutions step by step
- answer follow-up questions
- rephrase explanations in simpler language
- check student work
- generate similar practice problems
- graph supported functions and points
- keep chat sessions and conversation history
- export a chat transcript as a browser-openable `.html` file with LaTeX support

## Product Direction

The long-term goal is broader than math. Delphic AI should become a calm learning assistant that can support multiple subjects over time while keeping the same core style:

- clear reasoning
- short useful steps
- friendly follow-up support
- practice after explanation
- serious, mature interface

## Who It Is For

- students working through difficult questions
- tutors who want a focused learning assistant
- teachers exploring AI-supported learning workflows
- parents helping students practice at home
- education programs evaluating AI tutor prototypes

## Why It Is Different

Delphic AI is not trying to feel like a toy chatbot or a generic homework answer machine.

It is designed to:

- slow the student down
- explain why each step works
- keep the conversation connected
- encourage practice
- make the interface feel calm and usable

## Known Limits

- The app still depends on an external AI provider.
- Math verification is strongest for supported SymPy cases.
- Broader non-math subject support is a future direction, not a finished feature.
- Production use would need stronger authentication, privacy review, and monitoring.
